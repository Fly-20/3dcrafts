## 4. Video playbook: hooks that work for a maker business

**Rules:**
- The **first second** decides everything: movement plus on-screen text that creates a question.
- 7–20 s long; end on the **reveal**, ideally loopable back to the start.
- Captions should include searchable words ("3D printing Edinburgh", "custom keyring", "personalised wooden card"). TikTok and Instagram work like search engines now.
- Post 18:00–21:00 UK to start, then use your own analytics.

**Trending sounds:** scheduled or API posts generally **can't use in-app trending sounds**. For TikTok especially, post 1–2 videos a week **natively in the app** with a trending sound. HQ will send the finished video and caption to your phone so it takes about a minute.

**Hook formulas** (fill in from real jobs):

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| Problem → fix | "This £2 clip saved a £60 dishwasher." / "They stopped making this part, so we made it." |
| Customer challenge | "A customer sent me this sketch on a napkin…" → time-lapse → reveal |
| Time compression | "9 hours of printing in 9 seconds." |
| Guess what | "Guess what this is going to be." (people stay to the end to find out) |
| POV | "POV: you asked a 3D printer for a cake topper with your dog's face on it." |
| Before/after | Broken part next to new part, snap together |
| Satisfying process | Laser engraving close-up, supports peeling off, multicolour layer changes (no talking needed) |
| Price reveal | "What would you pay for this?" → answer in comments (drives comments) |
| Series | "Fixing Edinburgh one part at a time, ep. 12" / "Customer request of the week" |
| Behind the scenes | "How a wooden birthday card is made", start to finish |

**Content mix per week:** 2 customer jobs (with consent) · 1 problem→fix · 1 product/gift (seasonal) · 1 satisfying/process or behind the scenes.

## 5. Google Business Profile, reviews & SEO checklist

### 5.1 Google Business Profile (week 1, then weekly)

- [ ] **Categories:**
  - primary: *3D printing service*
  - secondary: *Engraver*, *Laser cutting service*, *Gift shop* (only if it fits how you trade)
- [ ] **Services** listed individually, each with a short description and "from £10" pricing: custom 3D printing, replacement parts, prototypes, laser engraving, personalised wooden cards, cake toppers, signage
- [ ] **Products:** 6–10 with photos (cards, keyrings, coaster set, custom print), linking to the website
- [ ] **Description** (750 characters) using the words people search for: 3D printing, laser engraving, Edinburgh, custom, replacement parts, personalised gifts
- [ ] **Photos:** 20+ now (workshop, P2S, laser in action, finished pieces, you). Then **3–5 new photos every week**.
- [ ] Hours, service area (Edinburgh + UK-wide delivery), website link with `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=gbp`, messaging on
- [ ] **One post every week** (Sunday batch): a time-lapse, a finished job or a seasonal offer
- [ ] **Holiday hours** and Christmas last order dates

### 5.2 Google reviews (+20 in 90 days)

- [ ] Get your **review short link** (GBP → "Ask for reviews") and save it in HQ settings
- [ ] Ask **every local, website and B2B customer** when they collect or receive the order, with a QR code on the time-lapse card for website orders
- [ ] Message **10–15 past happy customers you know personally or locally** this week and ask directly; this is the fastest way to start
- [ ] **Never** offer discounts or freebies for reviews, and never ask only happy customers. Both break Google's rules and can get reviews removed.
- [ ] Reply to **every** review within 48 h; mention the item naturally ("so glad the replacement bracket fitted")
- [ ] Etsy buyers: keep asking for **Etsy** reviews (Etsy ranking matters more for them); don't push them to Google

### 5.3 Local citations (week 2–3, one-off)

List the business with **identical name, address/area, phone and website** on: Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yell, FreeIndex, Scoot, Thomson Local, Cylex. Consistent listings help Maps ranking.

### 5.4 Website SEO (6 pages in 90 days)

Your site already has the basics: LocalBusiness structured data, product structured data, sitemap, robots and Search Console verification. Next:

| Page | Target searches | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| `/3d-printing-edinburgh` | 3D printing Edinburgh, 3D printing service Scotland | 6 Dec |
| `/laser-engraving-edinburgh` | laser engraving Edinburgh, personalised engraving | 13 Dec |
| `/replacement-parts` | 3D printed replacement part UK, discontinued part | 10 Jan |
| `/cake-toppers` (sector) | custom cake topper, cake topper for bakers | 24 Jan |
| Guide: *How much does 3D printing cost in the UK?* with an **instant estimate calculator** (uses the HQ pricing engine) | 3D printing cost UK, 3D print price calculator | 7 Feb |
| Guide: *What file do I need to get something 3D printed?* | STL file 3D printing service, how to get something 3D printed | 14 Feb |

Each page gets:
- real photos and an embedded time-lapse
- 3–5 FAQs
- "from £" pricing
- the quote form
- links to related pages

The cost calculator is the strongest asset: useful, likely to get linked to, and it creates enquiries.

**Links from other sites (ongoing, about 1 a month):**
- businesses you've made things for (bakers, cafés): ask for a "made by 3DCRAFTS" link
- Edinburgh directories and maker groups
- a Printables profile
- a local press pitch in January ("Edinburgh maker 3D prints parts manufacturers stopped making")
